ILLUSTRATIONS DE LA GALERIE
Une planche originale de six illustrations générées, recadrées pour le site. Les intitulés en surlignage blanc restent du texte HTML éditable.

Prompt de la planche
Create one carefully composed web illustration atlas, a landscape canvas with six rectangular illustrations in an exact 3 columns by 2 rows equal grid. The layout must have no padding or gaps and each rectangular illustration must fill its cell edge to edge. Each scene is centered inside its own cell with enough margin so objects do not touch the boundaries. This is for a very personal French book journal web site, Entre les lignes. Make the art feel like analog handmade picture-book illustration, pencil scribbles, irregular hand-cut colored paper, matte opaque gouache brush marks, visible rough paper grain, softly faded and perfectly imperfect, European independent picture-book editorial, nostalgic stationery. Palette dusty pale rose, powder blue, blush, mauve, apricot, soft mint, cream. No thick black line art, no vector look, no 3D, no smooth gradient, no fake handwriting, no letters or text anywhere. Six different scenes. Top left FICTION: a large open book with a miniature night landscape emerging from it, tiny houses, cloud and moon, dusty peach and dusty violet. Top center KNOWLEDGE: a mint antique magnifying glass, fern clipping, a star chart made with dots and tiny watercolor glass flasks on pale powder blue. Top right ARTS: wooden palette with several gouache dabs, fan of brushes, hand-cut flowing paper offcuts, dusty pink coral peach. Bottom left SOCIETY: little illustrated neighborhood with two small human silhouettes talking under a blooming tree, pale blue and blush. Bottom center SPIRITUALITY: moon phases and small stars over a lavender mountain landscape, on a muted deep rose lavender background, quietly dreamlike. Bottom right YOUTH: a tiny paper kite, pastel stars and a toy rabbit holding a blank little book, warm custard and peach. Full backgrounds in each cell, avoid white outer borders. Distinct background color per cell, enough texture and contrast. This will be cropped into six art-only illustrations with HTML captions placed over them later, so absolutely no typography, labels, watermarks, frames, or buttons.
